Chicago's world-class Museum Campus, Millennium Park and other popular areas are depicted on the map in big, bold text. Off shore, waves of text undulate through Lake Michigan. Get up close and see all of the neighborhood and street names in this part of the city intricately and colorfully woven together, including the Loop, Gold Coast, Michigan Avenue, and Lake Shore Drive.
